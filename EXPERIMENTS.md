**Numerical Experiments**

## Setup
- Rule #1.5
- Initial: 40 nodes, 25 hyperedges
- Up to 600–800 steps

## Observed Results
- The system grows steadily in nodes and hyperedges.
- Neutral clusters (δ = 0) become more prevalent.
- A transition balance near 1/137 appears in runs.
- Clusters show rudimentary stability and emergent properties.

These behaviors are real within the model, but their physical interpretation remains tentative.
