# PROOF_OF_CONCEPT.md

**Mathematical Framework and Emergent Properties**

## 1. Formal Setup

- Hypergraph $\mathcal{G} = (V, E)$
- 3-ary hyperedges with phase $\phi \in \{0,1,2\}$
- Local imbalance $\delta = (\sum \text{incident phases}) \mod 3$

## 2. Observed Emergent Behavior

Simulations demonstrate:
- Growth of the hypergraph
- Formation of increasingly stable neutral clusters ($\delta = 0$)
- Statistical stabilization of transition ratios (~1/137 in runs)

## 3. Correspondence with Physics (Tentative)

- Neutral clusters → possible analog of stable particles
- High-density regions → possible analog of black holes
- Global relaxation → possible analog of cosmic expansion

**Caveat**: These correspondences are interpretive. No rigorous limit to known physical equations has been established.

## 4. Future Work

- Larger scale simulations
- Better continuum limit analysis
- Investigation of whether the model can produce unique predictions

This document records current promising observations, not completed proofs.
