# MEMORANDUM: Emergent-Hypergraph-Physics (EHP)

**Date:** July 12, 2026
**Subject:** Transition to Discrete Computational Physics

## 1. The Paradigm Shift
The history of physics has been a long attempt to model reality using continuous mathematics (calculus, differential equations, real numbers). This has led to the "mathematical crisis" of modern physics: the incompatibility of General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics. 

EHP posits that this conflict arises from a false premise: that the universe is continuous. **We reject this.** We posit that the universe is a finite, deterministic computational process.

## 2. Engineering Principles for EHP
To ensure our model remains grounded in reality, every contributor must adhere to the following principles:

*   **1. Integer-First Logic:** If a calculation requires floating-point numbers, the model is likely flawed. Reality is discrete; therefore, our logic must be integer-based.
*   **2. Locality of Influence:** No action at a distance. All changes in the hypergraph must propagate node-by-node (the "speed of information" constraint).
*   **3. Topology over Geometry:** Do not define coordinates. Define connections. A position in space is defined only by its connectivity to other nodes.
*   **4. Minimalist Rewriting:** The complexity of the universe must emerge from the simplest possible set of rewriting rules. If a rule is complex, it is artificial.

## 3. The Objective
We are building a **Computational Simulator of Reality**. Our goal is to derive the observed laws of nature (Einstein’s Field Equations, Quantum behavior) as a side effect of a simple graph-rewriting algorithm.
